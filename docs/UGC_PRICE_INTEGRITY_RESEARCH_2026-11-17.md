# aStock / Roblox Price Tracker — Limited UGC Price-Integrity Investigation

**Research/retrieval date:** 2026-11-17 (UTC). **Codebase:** `3AYZE/Roblox-Price-Tracker`, v0.15.0 workstream. **Scope:** UGC creator primary pricing, secondary market asks and sale evidence, not an allegation that particular creators broke Roblox rules.

## Executive summary

Creator-listed original-stock prices and any creator-advertised "was/compare at" amounts are **seller-controlled**, not independent fair resale values. A resale ask below a creator's new listing price is therefore **not itself an investment discount**. Roblox's daily resale price/volume data and Rolimon's independently tracked resales are more appropriate secondary-market context, but RAP is a trailing average, resale asks are unexecuted offers, and resale volume does not establish the causal mechanism behind a price increase.

**Evidence standard matters:** In this public-source investigation, **zero named items have sufficient independently dated historical primary-price observations plus corroborating weak actual resale evidence to declare `EXCLUDED_PRICE_DISTORTION` as a verified real-world case**. Some show concerning price, inventory and resale-liquidity combinations and merit ongoing monitoring. The app's new exclusion rule is tested using clearly **synthetic** timelines, not by falsely assigning those facts to real assets.

One concrete warning: the named Angelic Valkyrie hair has conflicting snapshots—65 R$ original listing in an earlier Rolimon's capture; 350 R$ in a creator-store search index; and ~499 R$ cheapest resale ask in [RBX Invest's November 17, 2026 item report](https://rbxinvest.com/item/87368919368467). RBX Invest reports **365 R$ RAP**, 10 original copies remaining, and 2,895 recorded sales spanning 52 days, but explicitly says this combines creator-original purchases and actual player-to-player resales. It does **not** establish how many copies resold near the asking price or whether the creator currently charges 350 R$ to every region. The user reports 1,080–1,200 R$ creator/reference prices, **not independently reproduced**. No invented resale history is used.

### Verified price-distortion exclusions (public evidence)

**No real item is declared a confirmed manipulation/exclusion based solely on this public research.** A high creator price alone, even 25,000 R$, does not establish a historical price increase. A cheap reseller listing does not verify an executed sale or a market clearing price. Because independent dated early creator quotes and complete executed-sale records are missing for most of these items, a public-only verdict of `EXCLUDED_PRICE_DISTORTION` would overstate the evidence.

### Items meriting investigation

- **Angelic Valkyrie hair:** indexed 65 R$ and 350 R$ creator prices, contrasted with the Nov 17 reported 499 R$ resale ask and RAP of 365 R$. The separate executed resale subset of RBX Invest's 2,895 mixed sales is unknown; its creator-price description mentions inventory-linked repricing. Verify exact observation timestamps, acquisition region, source integrity and actual secondary-market volume before any hard exclusion. Reported 1,080/1,200 R$ was not independently reproduced.
- **Black Valk:** reported original-price field of 25,000 R$ versus resale asks in the hundreds; recover *chronological original-stock sales* to establish whether that field is an initial price, last creator price or a promotion.
- **Gold Premium Hammer, Limited U Chain, Gold Premium Valkyrie and Black Iron Clockwork Shades:** thin actual resale counts make asking prices weak estimators; avoid creator-price-based investment recommendations until meaningful completed resale demand is available.
- **Limited U Hammer:** RAP far above the lowest ask illustrates that RAP is not an executable bid.

### Items with more independent resale support

- **Christmas Candy Cane Valkyrie:** 298 tracked resales in a recent 7-day index is evidence of turnover; it does **not** prove wash trading is absent or that the 2,973 R$ ask is fair value.

## Meaningful comparable groups

1. **Winged blonde hair:** Angelic Valkyrie Winged Blonde Icarus Hair (3,000 total units) versus Sunlight UGC Valkyrie Winged Blonde Hair (2,500) share accessory type and winged hair aesthetic. The Sunlight item's original listing remained 65 R$ in a dated report; Angelic's earlier captured listing was also 65 R$. 2. **Back/hammer items by Sinister UGC's:** Gold Premium Hammer (1,000) versus Limited U Hammer (700) share broad format and creator but have different designs and very low monthly resale activity. Neither asking floor nor RAP has sufficient trading depth to infer a robust median fair market value.
3. **Valkyrie hat family:** Black Valk, Gold Premium Valkyrie, Limited U Valkyrie and Christmas Candy Cane Valkyrie are visually related but have different creators, stock, release timing, demand and secondary turnover. Do not pool their unadjusted prices or classify a higher-priced variant as manipulated simply because another variant is cheaper.
4. **Face accessories:** Red Rose and Black Iron Clockwork Shades are different appearance/use cases, so a same-asset-type match alone is insufficient for close pricing comps; require consistent appearance, supply and actual trade evidence.

## Resale holding period: essential false-positive guard

Roblox's [official support article](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313260-How-to-sell-items) and [Creator Hub documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) say that originally purchased Limiteds may have a **holding period of up to 30 days** before resale. Very low secondary turnover immediately after launch is therefore expected and is not proof that the creator has manipulated value. Public resale/price snapshots alone do not supply a reliable release-time-to-tradability timeline for every copy.

The app's conservative exclusion rule requires the earliest *verified original creator-price observation* to precede the current observation by at least 30 days before it can hard-exclude for creator-anchor distortion. A separate minimum of 7 dated resale-volume observations is required before claiming low completed resale demand. This is a **minimum observation-age guard**, not proof of the real launch date or that every copy is unlocked. More mature authenticated history and per-copy holding details would improve precision. During the age guard, return `INSUFFICIENT_PRICE_DATA` with reason `RESALE_HOLDING_PERIOD_UNMATURED`.

## Automatic screening policy implemented in the connected codebase

- Statuses: `PASSED`, `INSUFFICIENT_PRICE_DATA`, and `EXCLUDED_PRICE_DISTORTION`. Reason codes retain the explicit evidence conditions.
- **Trusted history:** append timestamped **Marketplace-verified original-stock** primary listing observations to local backed-up `ugc-primary-price-evidence.json` (max 36 points per asset, max 5,000 persisted points, defaults to 180-day horizon). Existing catalog-only anchors are **not** imported as verified evidence. No user cookies, new APIs or dependencies.
- **Creator repricing trigger:** at least two observations separated by default 15 minutes, current listing >=3× earlier verified creator listing, and increase >=100 R$. Creator ID and asset type must match. Hard exclusions additionally require at least 30 days between the first verified creator-price observation and the current observation (conservative resale-hold maturity guard).
- **Artificial bargain:** a resale ask <=80% of the newer creator price is merely one warning signal; not enough alone.
- **Independent corroboration:** known completed resale volume of at most 2/30 days **with at least 7 distinct observed volume-data days** (one zero day alone is not low demand), or at least 3 observed days of significantly lower daily average secondary sale prices with at least 3 transactions, or a sufficiently large and truly independent matched comparable resale median (>=3 comparables). The current app does not collect a broad independent comparables dataset; that signal is accepted by the modular evaluator but not populated with fabricated peers.
- **Demand override:** if independently observed 30-day resale volume is at least 8 and at least 3 observed daily prices meet 70% of current creator price, the historical repricing **does not automatically exclude** the item. This is a guardrail, not proof of fair value or freedom from suspicious trades.
- **Hard exclusion:** only large verified repricing + apparent creator-anchor discount + independent corroborating evidence yields `EXCLUDED_PRICE_DISTORTION`. Otherwise mark `INSUFFICIENT_PRICE_DATA` when the history or resale series cannot settle the issue.
- **Excluded behavior:** zero ranking/opportunity/entry scores, recommendation `EXCLUDED`, omitted from default primary-market deal filters and high-conviction totals, blocked from the Hunter paper-investment entry action; still visible when selecting the explicit research filter.
- **Config:** optional user-managed `ugc-price-integrity-rules.json` mirrors the documented property names in `UgcPriceIntegrityPolicy.Configuration`. Unsafe values revert to conservative defaults. Defaults are initial research thresholds, **not statistically optimized**.

## Limits, potential false positives and next validation

The 3× / 80% / 2-sales thresholds are **provisional**. Tests cover synthetic repricing, legitimate high-demand price rises, missing volume, near-instant corrections, category generalization, high supply and backed-up records. They are **not** a field-calibrated false-positive or false-negative rate. A real backtest requires a consented, timestamped longitudinal dataset covering creator price changes and *executed secondary trades* for many months, including reliable non-distorted control items.

Additional signals to evaluate when reliable data becomes available: time-local price-up/down cycles; multi-asset creator repricing correlated with stock changes; sales concentration across a small number of participants (only if permitted data exists); order-book depth versus executed transaction median; relevant comparable-item sell-through per available supply; and unusually stale or bursty resale volume. **Do not automatically classify "price rises as stock falls" as manipulation.** Do not confuse a resale-ask drop, normal regional pricing, seasonal demand, a genuine collector premium or a price correction with deception.
